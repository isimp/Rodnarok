# Limits

What Rodnarök knowingly leaves out or cannot do, by area.

Code: none of its own; each limit names the note it belongs to.

See also: all notes in [the index](index.md).

## Water and fish

A fish needs water as deep as its own swimming depth: the Tuna swims no shallower than 4 m, and no school comes where the water by the float is shallower than its fish swim. Whether the water allows a school is judged by the depth of the fish the bait drew for that cast, so a bait for deep fish, such as the Tuna, is turned away in more places. A school needs either open water 40 m beyond the float or water 4.5 m deep near it, so a lake both narrow and shallow gets none ([arrival](arrival.md)).

The Frosty bait is made from a Drake trophy, so the Northern Salmon is open from the Mountain on.

Forty metres and four metres under are judged out of sight; how far a fish shows depends on the water and the graphics settings. The 40 m are measured from every player loaded around the one fishing.

## Which game steers

Fish that come to a float belong to the game of the player fishing, and others see them as ordinary fish, with their signs. Only a fish of the world's own that the caster's game steers can be called; one another player's game steers is left to it, and a school comes instead where the water allows. The calling reach is the game's 50 m, so the fish called may be one the player had not seen; it swims in where it can be watched.

A fish taking line out is sent away from the rod only by the game that owns it, which for a school's fish is the game fishing; a world fish owned by another player's game takes the line out without heading away. The game's own break still holds: a fish dragging the float 10 m past the line, faster than the line goes out, breaks it as in the game ([the fight](fight.md)). The heave likewise relies on the game fishing owning the fish.

## Messages and levels

The too-little-water line shows only to the player who cast, and so does the line telling why a school was scared away: a player whose steps scared another's school is not told ([wary schools](wary.md)). A server's `Difficulty` is everyone's on it; a player picks a level of their own only in a world without a server that has Rodnarök ([difficulty](difficulty.md)).

## Looks

A seated player's rod moves by the seated pose and shows no heave ([sitting](sitting.md)).
