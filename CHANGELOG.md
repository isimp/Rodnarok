# Changelog

## 0.2.1

Five difficulty levels from `VeryEasy` to `VeryHard`, `Normal` playing as before. Picking a level fills in the settings it sets, and changing one of them yourself makes it `Custom`. Server setting `Difficulty`.

A school scared away for good tells you why: your steps or another player's, reeling through it, shyness close in or in shallow water, or a shot.

## 0.2.0

Plain *Fishing Bait* also brings the fish of the biome your float lands in, such as a *Trollfish* in the Black Forest; biome baits still bring only their own fish. Server setting `BiomeFishOnBait`.

Each fish shows a sign of its own on the water as it comes to your float, and only *Tuna* and *Northern salmon* still leap, mostly at dawn and dusk.

A worn *Wisplight*'s wisp searches round your float, dives in at a bite and comes back after, while your mist clearing stays with you. Your setting `FloatWisp`.

Each fish pulls the line by its own speed: a *Pike* or *Tuna* harder, an *Anglerfish* gentler. Server setting `PullBySpeed`.

A seated cast goes where you aim.

Fast fish such as *Pike* and *Tuna* now reach the hook instead of circling it, and a school still flinches when you reel quickly away from it.

## 0.1.1

A struggling fish takes line slower while the line still holds strain: short taps on the block button that keep the ring partly filled lose less line than letting go. Each struggle writes one log line: the line the fish took, eased off and held time, and any hold the game did not count.
