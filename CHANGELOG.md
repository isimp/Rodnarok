# Changelog

## 0.2.2

The signs of a fish coming to your float follow the school's way in instead of jumping between its fish, and the *Trollfish*'s wake trails where it swam. At the float they go quiet until one fish goes for the bait, which the sign then follows in.

The signs take the light of the hour and the weather, warm at dusk and dull in rain, with a bright crest over a darker trough, and far ones fade into the fog.

Your float twitches toward the fish going for the bait in its last moments, and once a fish is hooked or a school scared away the others dart off. A school holding a starred fish shows a little bolder, and a starred fish going for the bait shows bigger the more stars it has.

The signs ride the waves and drift with the wind. Calm water shows them clear and whole, rough water fainter and broken, so a *Serpent trophy*'s calm water reads best.

How strongly the signs show is a server setting, from half to twice as strong. Server setting `SignContrast`.

A hooked fish shows its next struggle coming. Your float lies still while there is time, grows restless in the last moments of a rest, and the line goes taut and quivers just before the fish bolts, later for a fast or starred fish. Through the struggle the line stays taut and shakes harder the closer it comes to snapping. Server setting `StruggleWarning`.

## 0.2.1

Five difficulty levels from `VeryEasy` to `VeryHard`, `Normal` playing as before. Picking a level fills in the settings it sets, and changing one of them yourself makes it `Custom`. Server setting `Difficulty`.

A school scared away for good tells you why: your steps or another player's, reeling through it, shyness close in or in shallow water, or a shot.

A bite no longer dips the float a second time while it can still be hooked.

## 0.2.0

Plain *Fishing Bait* also brings the fish of the biome your float lands in, such as a *Trollfish* in the Black Forest; biome baits still bring only their own fish. Server setting `BiomeFishOnBait`.

Each fish shows a sign of its own on the water as it comes to your float, and only *Tuna* and *Northern salmon* still leap, mostly at dawn and dusk.

A worn *Wisplight*'s wisp searches round your float, dives in at a bite and comes back after, while your mist clearing stays with you. Your setting `FloatWisp`.

Each fish pulls the line by its own speed: a *Pike* or *Tuna* harder, an *Anglerfish* gentler. Server setting `PullBySpeed`.

A seated cast goes where you aim.

Fast fish such as *Pike* and *Tuna* now reach the hook instead of circling it, and a school still flinches when you reel quickly away from it.

## 0.1.1

A struggling fish takes line slower while the line still holds strain: short taps on the block button that keep the ring partly filled lose less line than letting go. Each struggle writes one log line: the line the fish took, eased off and held time, and any hold the game did not count.
